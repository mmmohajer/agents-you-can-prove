# Product FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Open the account menu, choose Security, then Reset password. We email a link that expires in 30 minutes.

Q: What plans are available?
A: Standard, Plus, and Enterprise. Standard is self-serve. Plus adds priority support. Enterprise includes a named account manager and a custom refund window.

Q: When do I get charged?
A: We bill on the billing cycle start date printed on the invoice. For most Standard and Plus customers that is the same day of the month they signed up.

Q: How do I cancel?
A: Standard and Plus can cancel from Billing. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Enterprise cancellations go through the account manager.

Q: Do you have a mobile app?
A: Yes. iOS and Android. Features match the web app except batch exports, which are web-only.

Q: What is error E-4471?
A: E-4471 means the payment method was declined. Update the card under Billing and retry. It is not a product outage.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Plus and Enterprise can export from Settings, Data export. Standard customers can request a one-time export from support.

Q: How do I add a teammate?
A: Plus and Enterprise: Settings, Team, Invite. Standard is a single-seat plan and cannot add teammates.

Q: What languages does the product support?
A: English, Spanish, French, German, and Japanese in the UI. Support replies in English.

Q: Is there an API?
A: Plus and Enterprise have a REST API. Standard does not. API keys live under Settings, Developers.
